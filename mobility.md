### My Current Progress

I spend four weeks in Barcelona, this experience was particularly enriching thanks to the international team of volunteers I worked alongside, each bringing their own culture and way of doing things, which taught me a lot about adapting to different working styles. Since I didn't speak Spanish, I communicated in English throughout my stay, which pushed me out of my comfort zone and significantly improved my confidence speaking English day to day. Beyond the practical tasks, this kind of volunteering builds autonomy, teamwork, and the ability to find your bearings in a new environment with the language barrier being, by far, the biggest challenge to overcome.
