About me

I first discovered the diversity and possibilities of the electrical and electronic world during my BUT GEII, where I explored areas ranging from electronics and automation to embedded systems and digital signal processing. These experiences showed me how rich and varied this field is, and they sparked a strong interest in understanding and designing complex electrical systems.

Today, at ENSEEIHT, my goal is to continue developing my technical skills, broaden my knowledge, and progressively identify the sector that inspires me the most within the wide field of electrical and electronic engineering. I see these three years as an opportunity to grow, explore new domains, and strengthen both my technical and teamwork abilities.

This portfolio reflects my background, my evolution, and the direction I aim to take as I continue shaping my future as an engineer.

Mentors

SS

Sébastien SALAS

Director of Training at CAP’TRONIC

I had the opportunity to conduct an interview with Sébastien Salas, the Director of Training at CAP’TRONIC, an organization that supports companies in developing innovative electronic and embedded solutions. Although he is not an engineer himself, his long career in the field and his position gave him a broad, transversal understanding of the many professions linked to electronics, embedded systems, IoT, industrialisation and digital transformation.

This multidimensional perspective was extremely valuable for me. He explained the wide variety of roles that exist in the electronic and embedded sectors, from engineering to project supervision, cyber-security, industrialization and technical consulting. His experience allowed him to give me objective and insightful guidance regarding training paths, the differences between working in SMEs and large companies, and the importance of continuing to learn throughout an engineering career.

This discussion helped me better understand the diversity of possible directions within my field and encouraged me to keep my options open as I progress through my engineering studies. It reassured me in my choice to join an engineering school and confirmed that exploring different domains over the next three years will be essential to identify the sector that suits me best.
