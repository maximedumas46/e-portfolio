### Theme and Research Question

**Theme (E7):** Electric bikes and the decarbonisation of everyday travel. Key words: e-bike, modal shift, car, carbon footprint, fuel prices, road safety.

**Research question (E6):** *To what extent can the electric bike replace the car to decarbonise everyday travel?*

</section>

The five documents follow the structure taught in class: an informational source, two opposing press articles, an institutional source and a scientific source.

### Part 1 — The Promise (A5, A2, A1)

The e-bike can replace part of car travel. The scientific study (A5) estimates a high theoretical potential for CO₂ reduction, especially in rural and suburban areas. The press (A2) shows that rising fuel prices are starting to make this shift happen, with some households giving up a second car. The survey (A1) describes who actually rides: mostly older and rural users, motivated more by health and mobility than by climate concerns.

</article>
<article class="text-card prose" markdown="1">

### Part 2 — The Limits (A3, A4)

The opinion piece (A3) questions the green image of e-bikes: expensive batteries, motors that cannot be repaired, and production concentrated in China. The road-safety report (A4), the pivot document, shows that cycling deaths are rising, particularly outside urban areas — precisely where the e-bike's potential is highest.

</article>

### Critical Perspective

- **Shared interests:** the company Upway appears twice in the corpus (partner of A1, source in A2), and both press articles come from outlets with an interest in defending one side.
- **The missing piece:** the corpus does not cover *débridage* (illegally unlocking the speed limit) and non-compliant "fake e-bikes" sold online, which threaten both safety and the image of the e-bike (*60 Millions de consommateurs*, January 2026).

### What I Learned

This exercise taught me to assess a source before trusting it: who is behind it, what kind of document it is, and who might benefit from its message. As an engineer interested in electric mobility, it also showed me that a technically sound solution only succeeds if economic, safety and social conditions follow.
