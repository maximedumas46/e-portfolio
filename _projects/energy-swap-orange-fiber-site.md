### Objective

Replace and modernize the power equipment of an Orange fixed fiber site (rectifiers, batteries, DC48V distribution) to improve reliability, autonomy, and compliance with Orange standards. Collaboration with a Technical Manager.

- Analysis of existing energy infrastructure: rectifiers, batteries, DC board.
- On-site measurements and verification of constraints (safety, power, norms).

### Costing & Preparation

- Definition of required replacement equipment.
- Cost estimation and intervention time.

### SWAP Execution

- Removal of old power equipment.
- Installation of the new energy modules.
- DC wiring and commissioning tests.

### Final Validation

- Electrical measurements (voltage, current, battery autonomy).
- Update of technical documentation.

### Skills Developed

- Understanding of telecom electrical systems (DC48V).
- Technical project management (study → installation → validation).
