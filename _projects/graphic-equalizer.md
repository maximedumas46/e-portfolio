### Project Objective

Design and implement an audio graphic equalizer based on 2nd-order IIR digital filters, in order to modify the sound spectrum of a signal in real time.

### Achievements

- Theoretical study and design of filters (band-pass, high-pass, low-pass).
- Simulation of frequency response to validate filter coefficients.
- Implementation on Arduino for embedded digital processing.
- Experimental tests on an audio signal to verify the functionality of the different adjustments.

### Skills Developed

- Digital signal processing (IIR filters).
- Embedded programming (C / Arduino).
- Frequency analysis and experimental validation.
- Lab notebook documentation and methodological follow-up.
