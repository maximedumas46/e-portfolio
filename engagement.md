### Food Rescue & Animal Care — Barcelona

In August–September 2026, I spent four weeks volunteering in Barcelona, Spain, with ImpacTrip on the Food Rescue and Animal Care project.

I helped fight food waste and poverty by collecting unsold food from local restaurants, cafés, and supermarkets, and preparing personalized baskets for families in need. I also helped improve daily conditions at an overcrowded cat shelter, supporting the animals' health and well-being to give them a better chance of finding a home. Over 55 hours, this experience strengthened my sense of commitment and taught me a lot about teamwork outside an engineering context.
